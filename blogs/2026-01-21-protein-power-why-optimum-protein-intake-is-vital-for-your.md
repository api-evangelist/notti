As pet owners, we want to ensure that our furry companions are getting the best nutrition possible to live a happy and healthy life. Protein is essential for the overall health and wellbeing of dogs and cats. It helps in the growth and repair of muscles and tissues, promotes healthy skin and coat, supports immune function, and aids in the production of enzymes and hormones.
