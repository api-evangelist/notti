With so many commercial pet food options available on the market, it can be difficult to determine which ones are truly high quality and safe for our pets to consume. Many pet food brands use artificial additives and fillers to make their products more cost-effective and to extend their shelf life. However, these additives have been linked to a number of health problems in pets, including digestive issues, allergies, and even cancer.
