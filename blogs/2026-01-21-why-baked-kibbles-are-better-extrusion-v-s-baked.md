When it comes to feeding our furry friends, there are a variety of options available, including kibbles. Kibbles are dry pet food that are made using various methods, the two most common being extrusion and baking. While both methods have their own advantages and disadvantages, slow baked kibbles are gaining popularity due to their many benefits.
